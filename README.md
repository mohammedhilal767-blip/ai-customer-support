# AI-Assisted Customer Support

This project shows how AI improves customer responses.

---

## Example 1

Customer:
"I’m really upset, my product is damaged."

Basic Response:
"Sorry for the issue."

Improved Response:
"Hi, I’m really sorry to hear your product arrived damaged. I completely understand how frustrating this must be. I’ll arrange a replacement or refund for you right away. Could you please share your order number so I can assist you quickly?"

---

## Example 2

Customer:
"No one is replying to me."

Basic Response:
"We will respond soon."

Improved Response:
"Hi, I sincerely apologize for the delay in our response. Thank you for your patience. I’m here now and ready to assist you. Could you please share more details about your issue?"
